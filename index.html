IBJA Events, the iPhone app

71 screens in 12 flows, in the order a delegate meets them, from the first launch to the ad spaces. Please approve each one, or say what should change. Tap a screen to see it larger.

Sample content: apart from the signed-in IBJA user, delegates, speakers and their companies are made up. Sponsor and exhibitor names and logos are IBJA Verified members shown as placeholders. The sponsorships, stalls, offers and messages on these screens are not real.

How to review

  1. Enter your name once.
  2. Under each screen, press Approve, or Needs change and write what should change.
  3. It saves as you go. You can stop and come back on this device.
